# Privacy Policy — Ember (Pomodoro Timer)

_Last updated: June 11, 2026_

Ember is a pomodoro timer Chrome extension. This policy describes what
data it handles. The short version: **Ember collects nothing.**

## Data collection

Ember does **not** collect, transmit, sell, or share any data. It has no
analytics, no accounts, no ads, and makes no network requests. It
requests no host permissions, so it cannot read or modify any website.

## Data stored on your device

Ember keeps a small amount of data in Chrome's local extension storage
(`chrome.storage.local`), only so the timer works:

- **Timer state** — current phase, remaining time, and whether the timer
  is running.
- **Settings** — your chosen durations, auto-start, sound, and
  notification preferences.
- **Daily session counts** — the number of focus sessions and minutes
  completed per day, used for the "today" tally shown in the timer.

This data never leaves your machine. It is removed by Chrome when you
uninstall the extension, and you can clear it at any time from
`chrome://extensions` → Ember → "Clear data", or by removing and
reinstalling the extension.

## Permissions

- **alarms** — schedules when a focus session or break ends.
- **storage** — saves the settings and timer state described above.
- **notifications** — shows an optional desktop notification when a
  phase ends.
- **offscreen** — plays the optional end-of-phase chime (service workers
  cannot play audio).
